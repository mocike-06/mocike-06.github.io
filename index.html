范文一：The Importance of Lifelong Learning 议论文 · 高频
Lifelong learning means the continuous pursuit of knowledge throughout one's life. In today's rapidly changing world, it has become more important than ever. First of all, lifelong learning keeps us competitive in the job market. New technologies appear every day, and workers who fail to update their skills may be left behind. By learning new things, we can adapt to the changing demands of our careers. Secondly, learning enriches our inner world. Reading books, taking online courses and exploring new hobbies all broaden our horizons and make life more meaningful. It is a good way to relax and grow at the same time. In conclusion, we should view learning not as a task that ends with graduation, but as a lifelong habit. Only by keeping learning can we keep up with the times and live a fuller life.
范文二：Should College Students Take Part-time Jobs 议论文 · 高频
Whether college students should take part-time jobs has been widely discussed. In my opinion, doing part-time work is beneficial if it is well managed. On the one hand, part-time jobs offer students a chance to gain practical experience. Working in a restaurant or doing tutoring helps them learn how to communicate with different people and how to manage their time efficiently. Besides, the income can ease the financial burden on their families. On the other hand, we cannot ignore the risks. If students spend too much time on work, their study may be affected. Some students even feel tired and stressed because they fail to balance work and study. Therefore, I believe students should choose part-time jobs carefully and keep a proper balance between work and study. Only in this way can they benefit from the experience without hurting their academic performance.
翻译一：中国茶文化传统文化
中国是茶的故乡，饮茶在中国有着悠久的历史。茶不仅是一种饮品，更是一种文化。中国人喜欢在闲暇时与朋友一起品茶、聊天。如今，中国茶已经走向世界，受到越来越多外国朋友的喜爱。
参考译文：China is the hometown of tea, and drinking tea has a long history in China. Tea is not only a drink but also a kind of culture. Chinese people enjoy tasting tea and chatting with friends in their spare time. Today, Chinese tea has gone to the world and is loved by more and more foreign friends.
翻译二：大学生活校园生活
大学生活是人生中一段宝贵的时光。在这里，我们不仅学习专业知识，还结交了来自全国各地的朋友。图书馆、操场和宿舍都留下了我们的美好回忆。毕业后，我们会带着这些经历走向更广阔的天地。
参考译文：College life is a precious period in one's life. Here, we not only learn professional knowledge but also make friends from all over the country. The library, the playground and the dormitory are all filled with our beautiful memories. After graduation, we will carry these experiences into the wider world.
